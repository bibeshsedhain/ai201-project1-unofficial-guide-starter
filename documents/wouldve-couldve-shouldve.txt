Looking back, Veronica would consider testing out of some General Institute Requirements so that the freed time could go to exploring academic departments before declaring a major.

# First-year communities
Veronica knew students who enjoyed Terrascope and DesignPlus and sees these communities as low-pressure ways to meet classmates and faculty. DesignPlus staff let Veronica join friends at weekly lunches despite being outside the group.

# Advisor's referrals
When Veronica was uncertain about a major, the first-year advisor provided contacts in Course 8 and Course 18. Veronica did not follow up and later wished they had explored these options before declaring Course 2, Mechanical Engineering.

# Workload priorities
Veronica prioritized learning well, sleeping a reasonable amount, and having time outside classes. The extensive Course 2 requirements made it harder to fit in exploratory classes.

# Hands-on growth
The author ultimately enjoyed challenging practical work in Mechanical Engineering. Working outside their comfort zone helped Veronica better appreciate what it takes to make a design work.
