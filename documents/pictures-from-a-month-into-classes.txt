# Social Innovation Exploration
Richard joined Social Innovation Exploration before orientation because of an interest in food accessibility. During the program, the group learned about food insecurity on campus and around Boston and visited Boston Haymarket.

# DesignPlus vertical garden
Richard describes learning applied design through DesignPlus, a first-year learning community. The author was working with other students on a vertical garden project in Haiti.

# First-term grading
Richard says first-year Pass/No Record helped students get used to academics. The post describes a passing grade appearing as P on the transcript and a failed class disappearing from it.

# Student communities
Food-accessibility peers, the DesignPlus cohort, and the Chocolate City living community helped Richard find a place at MIT. Richard describes exploring makerspaces with the DesignPlus group.

# Social breaks during midterms
Richard recalls a bake-off between the Chocolate City and Juniper living communities during midterm study. The event gave the author a break and a chance to spend time with friends.
