# Calculus placement
Yuliya tried three versions of multivariable calculus in the first semester. The author started in 18.014, transferred to 18.022, and then settled on 18.02. The experience is presented as a reason to adjust levels when the pace or format is a poor fit.

# Balancing technical subjects
Yuliya's first schedule packed in technical GIRs to take advantage of Pass/No Record. The plan felt overloaded within a month, and the author worked with an advisor to shed courses or move down a level. Yuliya advises leaving room to explore interests.

# Switching levels
Yuliya advises contacting the instructor of the class you are moving into so they can tell you whether you need to make up material. The post gives physics and chemistry level changes as examples alongside the author's calculus changes.

# Physical education
The post describes popular PE sections filling quickly and mentions an early registration time for the author's year. Yuliya suggests showing up for the first session when waitlisted in case an enrolled student does not attend.

# Finding support
Yuliya says a good relationship with the first-year advisor can help with schedules, extracurricular workload, and research leads. The post also identifies Student Support Services as a resource when a student runs into difficulty.
