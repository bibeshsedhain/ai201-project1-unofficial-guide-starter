# Terrascope project
Suzuko describes Terrascope as a first-year learning community for environmental sustainability. The cohort's group project centered on water scarcity and heat waves in the Navajo Nation.

# Group project transition
Suzuko sees Terrascope as a continuation of their Discover Course 1 pre-orientation experience because both involve group work on environmental problems. The author expected to grow by tackling open-ended problems with a group.

# Learning about transportation
During Discover Course 1, a module on transportation game theory had student teams take on roles such as public transportation authorities and bike-share companies.

# Building model turbines
A Discover Course 1 activity used cardstock-like paper and foam to make small wind turbines after researchers discussed modeling wind flows computationally.

# Makerspace access
The author describes visiting Metropolis, a makerspace in the basement of Building 4. A mentor helped the team adapt a treadmill model and print it on a 3D printer. The post describes doing some training before using the space.
