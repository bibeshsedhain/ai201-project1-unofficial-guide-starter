# Long-term course planning
The author uses CourseRoad to sketch several possible multi-year class plans. FireRoad combines functions of Firehose and CourseRoad, and the author describes it as a way to lay out multiple years and see projected class times.

# Looking up workload
MIT Subject Evaluations can help students compare courses. The post describes overall ratings and reported weekly homework hours as two pieces of information available for recent subjects.

# Major requirements
MIT Degree Charts list major requirements and can help students decide which undergraduate electives to explore. Department websites also sometimes show suggested paths for getting through a program.

# Course catalog
The blogger finds the course catalog useful for listing subjects in a department subfield once a student understands MIT's numbering system. The post describes it as less beginner-friendly than the other planning tools.

# Advice from people
The author finds discussions with an advisor or more experienced students in a major especially helpful. The author spent considerable time planning a path to graduation after changing majors.
