# Working together
The author recommends finding one or two people in each class to work through problem sets and ask questions with. Class group chats and a friend in each subject helped the author avoid working through everything alone.

# Help from instructors
When work stalls, the author recommends going to office hours or tutorials. Specific examples include Sunday help sessions for 8.01 and asking for help in 6.0001 after spending hours on code that would not compile.

# Advanced Standing Exams
The author suggests attempting an Advanced Standing Exam when students already know the material well enough to benefit more from another course. Getting credit can leave room to explore a major. The author did not earn credit for 18.01 or 8.01 and ended up valuing the math and physics foundation.

# Early classes
The author warns against filling a schedule with 9 a.m. classes based on an optimistic view of one's sleep habits. Students should choose early classes based on when they can realistically get up.

# First-term perspective
The author sees Pass/No Record as breathing room to adjust to college, build friendships, and look for activities outside class. The advice draws on a first-year student's own experience of the transition.
