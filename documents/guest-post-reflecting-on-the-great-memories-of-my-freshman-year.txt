# ESG community
The blogger joined the Experimental Study Group (ESG) and took General Institute Requirements in small classes alongside other ESG students. The post mentions Friday community lunches cooked by students and a shared space where first-years could spend time.

# Fall and spring physics
The blogger took 8.012, a proof-oriented mechanics course, in the fall through ESG. The same five students stayed together and took 8.022 in the spring with the same professor in the same room.

# Physics study group
The blogger often worked on physics problem sets with the four other 8.012 students. They also started getting a meal together every week and got to know one another outside class.

# Office hours in ESG
During a busy week, the professor held office hours after 8.012. The students were about to skip the session for lunch, so the professor prepared food in the ESG kitchen while the group stayed to work.

# International orientation
The blogger advises incoming international students to meet as many other students as possible at international orientation. The writer recalls arriving ahead of the rest of the class and doing activities to get comfortable living in the United States.
